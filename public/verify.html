// How it works

A simple answer backed by
mathematical certainty

April Gate is designed so that insurers, lawyers, compliance officers, and judges can trust the result — without understanding the underlying technology. Here is what happens, in plain terms.

1
Each sensor carries a unique, unforgeable identity

Every April Gate sensor contains a tamper-resistant chip — similar to the chip inside a credit card or a passport — that holds a private cryptographic key. That key is burned into the hardware at manufacture and cannot be extracted, copied, or transferred. Every temperature reading the sensor produces is signed by that key.

In court terms: the sensor cannot lie about its identity, the same way a passport chip cannot be cloned.
2
Multiple independent sensors must agree

A shipment carries several sensors placed independently throughout the container. They continuously cross-check each other's readings. If one sensor is tampered with, removed, or replaced — the others detect the disagreement. A single compromised sensor cannot corrupt the record.

In court terms: independent witnesses who cannot coordinate a false story.
3
A tamper-evident proof chain is written on-chain — not a log

April Gate does not store thousands of individual temperature readings in a database that someone could alter. Instead, each proof is linked to the one before it in a hash chain anchored on Solana. Altering, removing, or reordering any entry breaks the chain — and the break is detectable by anyone. Raw operational data stays encrypted off-chain, under the customer's own key.

In court terms: a tamper-evident record whose integrity anyone can re-check, independently.
4
The certificate is recorded permanently on blockchain

The mathematical certificate is written to the Solana blockchain — a global, distributed ledger that no single person, company, or government controls. Once written, it cannot be altered, deleted, or backdated. April Gate itself cannot change it. The shipment company cannot change it. It is as permanent as any record can be.

In court terms: a notarized document held simultaneously by thousands of independent parties worldwide.
5
Anyone can verify the result — instantly, for free

Enter the shipment ID above. April Gate checks the blockchain record and returns a simple answer: valid or not valid. No account required. No fee. No interpretation needed. The same result is returned to the insurer, the pharma company, the regulator, and the judge — because it comes from the same immutable source.

In court terms: public record that all parties can independently confirm.

Can April Gate alter the records after the fact?
No. Once a proof is written to the Solana blockchain, it is outside April Gate's control entirely. The blockchain is maintained by thousands of independent computers worldwide. No single party — including April Gate — can alter, delete, or add to an existing record. This is the fundamental property that makes the system trustworthy.
What if a sensor fails or connectivity is lost during transit?
Connectivity gaps are expected during ocean freight and are recorded as expected gaps — distinguishable from suspicious gaps caused by deliberate interference. Sensors buffer readings locally and submit them when connectivity returns. A tamper attempt that disconnects a sensor looks very different from an ocean transit dead zone, and both are recorded immutably on-chain.
Is this admissible as evidence in court?
Blockchain evidence is already accepted in multiple jurisdictions including the United States, China, and across the European Union. April Gate's records are designed to meet the standards of electronic evidence — with an unbroken chain of custody from physical sensor to blockchain record. As with any expert evidence, an expert witness would present the methodology to the court.
Does April Gate have access to the raw temperature data?
Raw temperature data is stored off-chain, encrypted with the customer's own encryption key. April Gate does not hold the decryption key and cannot access the raw data. The on-chain record contains only the mathematical proof — which confirms conditions were met without revealing proprietary details such as exact readings, timing, or routing information.
How does the proof chain prove a record wasn't altered?
Each proof is cryptographically linked to the one before it: every entry includes a fingerprint of the previous entry, all the way back to the shipment's origin. Change, remove, or reorder any single record and every fingerprint after it stops matching — so tampering can't be hidden. Anyone can recompute the chain from the public on-chain data and confirm it for themselves, without trusting April Gate. Raw operational details stay encrypted off-chain, under the customer's own key.
