01 · The $35 Billion Loss

"The biopharma industry loses approximately $35 billion annually as a result of failures in temperature-controlled logistics."

IQVIA Institute for Human Data Science — 2019 Biopharma Cold Chain Logistics Survey
Published by Pelican BioThermal. Includes lost product, clinical trial loss and replacement costs, wasted logistics costs, and root-cause analysis costs.
cdn2.hubspot.net/.../PEL1046_SurveyReport_v4a.pdf
Air Cargo News — July 2019
"Failures in temperature-controlled logistics cost biopharma industry billions." Trade-press coverage of the IQVIA figure.
aircargonews.net/sectors/pharma-logistics/failures-in-temperature-controlled-logistics
Note on scope. The $35B figure is biopharma-specific, not all cold chain. Other industry sources give a range of $20B–$35B.

02 · Vaccine Wastage

"More than 50% of vaccines may be wasted globally every year, in part due to temperature control, logistics, and shipment-related issues."

World Health Organization
Original WHO publication (2005), reiterated across subsequent Effective Vaccine Management (EVM) materials and WHO immunization supply chain documentation.
who.int/teams/immunization-vaccines-and-biologicals/.../supply-chain
UN Environment Programme — June 2020
"Why optimized cold-chains could save a billion COVID vaccines." Quantifies the WHO figure at COVID-rollout scale.
unep.org/news-and-stories/story/why-optimized-cold-chains-could-save-billion-covid-vaccines
World Economic Forum — 2018
"Over half of vaccines are wasted globally for these simple reasons."
weforum.org/stories/2018/07/the-biggest-hurdle-to-universal-vaccination-might-just-be-a-fridge
